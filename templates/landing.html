How the Decision Support System Works

A seamless three-stage cyber-physical loop connecting microclimate sensing, machine learning prediction, and automated execution.

1
Sense & Simulate

Physical ESP32 IoT sensors or the background physics engine capture continuous temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and light telemetry.

2
Predict & Analyze

Scikit-Learn ML pipelines process polynomial interactions and ensemble algorithms to compute precise water liters and fertilizer dosage.

3
Automate & Protect

Intelligent relay controllers trigger ventilation fans, pumps, or alarm buzzers while enforcing closed-loop pot overflow protection.
