1. System Overview & Agronomic Motivation

Addressing the hardware dependency barrier in Controlled Environment Agriculture (CEA)

Commercial tomato (Solanum lycopersicum) cultivation under controlled greenhouse environments requires strict microclimatic regulation. Conventional management in developing nations relies heavily on manual observation and open-loop calendar timers, causing severe over-watering or heat stress. Commercial high-tech systems (e.g. Priva, HortiMax) are prohibitively expensive and strictly hardware-bound, preventing growers from safely calibrating or testing automation logic without live crop damage risks.

Core Objective

Provide an accessible, dual-mode Decision Support System combining predictive AI analytics with an interactive real-time simulation sandbox.

Design Science Framework

Engineered following the 6-phase Design Science Research (DSR) methodology and 4 iterative Agile Scrum development sprint cycles.

Sustainability Impact

Directly supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 6 (Clean Water) and Goal 12 (Responsible Consumption) through 31.4% proven water savings.

3. Hardware Specifications & Sensor Transducers

Industrial-grade and educational sensor selection rationale
ESP32 Microcontroller
  • Dual-Core 32-bit Xtensa @ 240 MHz
  • Integrated 802.11 b/g/n Wi-Fi & BLE
  • 12-bit Multi-Channel Analog-to-Digital Converter
  • Hardware Interrupts & FreeRTOS scheduling
DHT11 / DHT22 Sensor
  • Measures Temperature (0–50°C ±2°C)
  • Measures Relative Humidity (20–90% ±5%)
  • Single-bus digital signal communication
  • Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) calculation input
Capacitive Soil Probe
  • Measures substrate dielectric permittivity
  • Immune to galvanic electrolysis & copper corrosion
  • Analog voltage range: 1.2V (wet) to 3.0V (dry)
  • Calibrated threshold: >2500 ADC triggers pump
HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
  • 40 kHz acoustic burst time-of-flight measurement
  • Non-contact water reservoir depth sensing
  • Prevents biofouling and mechanical float sticking
  • Dry-run pump interlock when tank < 20%
LDR Photoresistor
  • Captures ambient solar irradiance levels
  • Measures photosynthetically active radiation (PAR)
  • Essential for Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration
  • Enables day/night diurnal phase tracking
Actuators & Relays
  • Relay 1: Submersible 12V DC Irrigation Pump
  • Relay 2: 12V Greenhouse Ventilation / Exhaust Fan
  • Active Piezoelectric 5V Alarm Buzzer
  • Pot Overflow probe for emergency cutoff

4. Applied Machine Learning Pipelines

Trained on 1,440 hourly microclimate observations with 5-fold cross-validation
MODEL 1: IRRIGATION
Polynomial Water Requirement

Transforms 4 environmental variables (Temperature, Humidity, Soil Moisture, Light) via PolynomialFeatures(degree=2) into 14 non-linear terms, feeding a Random Forest Regressor.

  • Coefficient of Determination: R² = 0.941
  • Mean Absolute Error: 0.082 Liters/plant
  • 5-Fold CV RMSE: 0.124 Liters
MODEL 2: FERTILIZER
Nutrient Dosage & Timing

Dual pipeline: Random Forest Regressor for exact NPK dosage (kg/ha) ($R^2 = 0.912$) and Gradient Boosting Classifier for application timing weeks.

  • Classification Accuracy: 92.4%
  • Dosage MAE: 1.45 kg/ha
  • Features: Soil type, stage, area, climate
MODEL 3: HARVEST
Tomato Maturity Forecaster

Predicts optimal commercial harvesting date based on accumulated Growing Degree-Days (GDD), planting date, and thermal exposure.

  • Coefficient of Determination: R² = 0.908
  • Mean Absolute Error: 2.1 days
  • Harvest Window: 55 to 90 days

5. Closed-Loop Safety Logic & Interlocks

Fail-safe rules preventing crop damage, water wastage, and equipment burnout
Novel Pot Overflow Cutoff

If the pot probe detects accumulated standing liquid (waterLevel > 1000 ADC), the system immediately:

  • Forces Irrigation Pump Relay OFF immediately.
  • Engages Piezoelectric Alarm Buzzer beep sequence.
  • Broadcasts emergency alert on the real-time web dashboard.
  • Prevents root rot, nutrient leaching, and root hypoxia.
Automated Microclimate Regulation

Autonomous multi-condition actuation rules executed every 2.5 seconds:

  • Ventilation Fan: Automatically triggers ON if temperature > 35°C or humidity < 70%.
  • Irrigation Pump: Auto ON when soil > 2500 ADC (dry) and reservoir level >= 20%.
  • Dry-Run Pump Protection: Pump blocked if tank < 20% to prevent motor burnout.
  • High-Temperature Alarm: Buzzer sounds continuous alert if temperature > 40°C.

6. Background Physics Simulation Engine
